by Main St Audio Labs
Version 1.0.0 (Release)

===============================================================================
1. ABOUT MIDNIGHT RAMBLER
===============================================================================
Midnight Rambler is a virtual stompbox amplifier emulation based on the 
legendary 1950s Fender Tweed Deluxe 5E3 — the holy grail of recording amps 
in rock history, famous for its organic tube compression and touch response.

Features:
- Dual Channel Neural Tube Engine: EDGE (Breakup) & CRANKED (Overdrive)
- Mix-Ready Studio Suite: Post-amp State-Variable TPT Bass (HP) & Tone (LP) filters
- Zero-Latency Speaker Cabinet Convolution: 1971 Oxford 12" (SM57 & R121 mics)
- Adaptive Input Noise Gate (-58 dBFS) & Pre-NAM Analog Waveshaper Limiter
- 0% CPU Standby Switch & Drag-and-Drop Dynamic Background Skins

===============================================================================
2. QUICK INSTALLATION GUIDE
===============================================================================

--- WINDOWS ---
- VST3 Plugin: Copy `MidnightRambler.vst3` to your VST3 folder:
  `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\`
- Standalone App: Run `MidnightRambler.exe` directly (Make sure to select 
  ASIO drivers in audio options for lowest latency).

--- macOS ---
- VST3 Plugin: Copy `MidnightRambler.vst3` to:
  `/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/`
- Audio Unit (AU): Copy `MidnightRambler.component` to:
  `/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/`
- Standalone App: Copy `MidnightRambler.app` to your Applications folder.

--- LINUX ---
- VST3 Plugin: Copy `MidnightRambler.vst3` to:
  `~/.vst3/` or `/usr/lib/vst3/`
- Standalone App: Run `MidnightRambler` binary.
